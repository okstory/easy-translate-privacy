隐私政策 / Privacy Policy 生效日期：2026年2月28日 1. 核心原则 / Core Principle 易翻译 极其重视您的隐私。本应用的设计初衷是提供纯净、安全的本地实时翻译体验。 Easy Translate values your privacy. Our app is designed to provide a pure, secure, and local real-time translation experience. 2. 权限使用说明 / Permissions Usage 屏幕录制权限 (Screen Recording): 本应用请求此权限**仅用于获取系统级别音频流（System Audio）**以进行实时翻译。声明：我们绝对不会录制、捕获、存储或传输您的屏幕图像或视频画面。 Screen Recording Permission: This app requests this permission solely to capture system-level audio streams for real-time translation. Statement: We strictly DO NOT record, capture, store, or transmit any screen images or video frames. 语音识别权限 (Speech Recognition): 用于将捕获的音频转换为文字。该过程优先调用系统内置引擎。 Speech Recognition Permission: Used to convert captured audio into text. This process prioritizes the use of the system's built-in engine. 3. 数据处理与传输 / Data Processing & Transmission 本地处理 (Local Processing): 所有的音频分析、文字识别和翻译过程均在您的设备本地完成。 Local Processing: All audio analysis, speech recognition, and translation processes are performed locally on your device. 不进行上传 (No Data Upload): 本应用绝不会将您的原始音频数据、识别后的文本或翻译内容上传至任何未经授权的服务器或第三方机构。 No Data Upload: This app NEVER uploads your raw audio data, recognized text, or translated content to any unauthorized servers or third parties. 4. 数据保留 / Data Retention 本应用不存储任何转换后的文本或音频文件。一旦翻译会话结束或窗口清空，相关瞬时数据将立即从内存中释放。 The app does not store any converted text or audio files. Once the session ends or the window is cleared, the transient data is immediately released from memory.
